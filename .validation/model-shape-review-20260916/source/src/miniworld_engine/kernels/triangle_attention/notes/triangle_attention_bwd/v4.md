# triangle_attention — v4: make_block_ptr stride-native + contiguous bias (SHIP, 1.50× vs baseline)

**GPU:** H100 (sm90).  **Shape:** d_pair=512, L=384, n_head=4 → HEAD_DIM=128, B=1, bf16.
**Base:** v2 (FA2 split, 8.36 ms/layer).  **Baseline:** 10.98 ms/layer.

## Verdict

**SHIP.** Consume q/k/v as the module's strided transpose views (no `.contiguous()` copy)
via `tl.make_block_ptr`, while keeping **bias contiguous**. Correct (cos 0.99999 / 0.99998)
and **fwd+bwd step 8.36 → 7.32 ms (1.14× vs v2, 1.50× vs baseline)**.

## Key finding — the pair-bias, not q/k/v, was the whole stride penalty

Earlier rounds concluded "strided is slower, `.contiguous()` is necessary." That was WRONG,
caused by two confounds: (1) raw-pointer strided loads are buggy (cos 0.75, not a stride law),
(2) both q/k/v AND bias were strided. Isolation (fixed make_block_ptr forward, per-input
contiguity sweep):

| q/k/v | bias | correct? | fwd time |
|---|---|---|---|
| contig | contig | yes | 0.73 ms |
| strided | strided | yes (bp) | 1.71 ms |
| **strided** | **contig** | **yes** | **0.72 ms** ← same as all-contig |
| contig | strided | yes | 1.68 ms |

So **q/k/v strided is FREE** (head_dim is stride-1 → coalesced), exactly as FA theory says.
The 2.3× penalty was **entirely the bias**: its transpose view has key-dim stride = n_head
(=4), a fully-gappy non-coalesced load. Fix: `bias.contiguous()` (tiny, ~1 MB) and leave
q/k/v strided (skip the ~450 MB q/k/v transpose copies).

Two requirements to realize it:
1. **make_block_ptr** loads for q/k/v — the existing raw-pointer addressing computes wrong
   results on strided input (cos 0.75); block-ptr is correct (cos 0.999998) and lets the
   compiler emit efficient strided loads.
2. **contiguous bias** (done in `Function.forward`).

## Implementation

- `_attn_fwd`, `_attn_bwd_dkdv`, `_attn_bwd_dq` rewritten with `tl.make_block_ptr`; q/k/v use
  strided 5D strides `(qs_z,qs_h,qs_lrow,qs_tok,qs_d)`, o-group (out/do/dq/dk/dv) contiguous,
  bias contiguous and broadcast over the query-row.
- Module: drop `.contiguous()` on query/key (value already strided). `Function.forward` does
  `bias.contiguous()` and allocates contiguous out/m; saves strided q/k/v.
- `_attn_bwd_dkdv` (v4b): keep tl.trans count low (2/iter, in the [key,query] space like the
  raw-ptr v2 kernel) — a naive make_block_ptr dkdv with 4 trans/iter was a WASH (8.36 ms);
  cutting to 2 trans recovered dkdv and turned it into the win.
- Precision unchanged (fp32 accum, bf16 storage).
