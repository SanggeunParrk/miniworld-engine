## Completion status

Facts as of 2026-09-28 (v2.2.0). Rules: [README](../README.md#completion-tables).

A100 runs Triton everywhere except the fused sm_80 Transition (D128, n=4, bf16) and the sm_80
TriMul (D128, bf16, B = 1, L % 16 == 0), the two sm_80 hand-CUDA paths wired into dispatch. A tuned cache exists
(`src/miniworld_engine/autotune/data/*/NVIDIA A100 80GB PCIe (sm80).json`, 72 ops) but it
was built under torch 2.10 / triton 3.6 and is stale on the 2.2.0 environment until rebuilt.
The six `sm80 cuda` registry rows are native LayerNorm/norm kernels.

## TriMul sm80

Module-level (`TriangleMultiplication` / `BidirectionalTriangleMultiplication`, `implementation=miniworld`), A100 80GB PCIe
(300 W), torch 2.13 / triton 3.7, CUDA-graph replay, median of 7 × 20; the fixture of `experiments/a100_trimul_fwd` (B = 1, bf16,
10 % masked tokens, dropout 0.25 with a fixed row scale in training). Triton = the same module with `MINIWORLD_TRIMUL_SM80=0`.
Measured 2026-09-30, job 59538 (gpu01). Anthropic and cuEquivariance are the branch records (torch 2.10, not re-measured on 2.2.0;
Anthropic ships no backward). SoL = the composite floor of the decomposition (`experiments/a100_trimul_fwd` README: 1.60 TB/s,
240 TFLOP/s measured on this card).
