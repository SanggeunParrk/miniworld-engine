## 4. Bottleneck theory
- Classification: **mixed** — dominated by memory-bound elementwise + memory-bound transpose, plus low-occupancy GEMMs.
- Evidence:
  - Largest chunk (27%, 468 us) is the tm1 gate pointwise: `torch.sigmoid(gate_buf)` (allocates a temp) then `mul_`. That is 4 full passes (2 sigmoid + 2 mul) over 128M-element bf16 [B,D,L,L] tensors. ncu: sigmoid pass at 82% mem-io = memory-bound; the work is redundant (a fused `proj*sigmoid(gate)` needs 1 read proj + 1 read gate + 1 write vs sigmoid(read+write) then mul(read+read+write)).
  - ln_out `layer_norm_transpose` at 90% mem-io = memory-bound (dbn->bnd transpose of [d,b,L*L]), 302 us / 17%.
  - tm1 GEMMs and tm2 run at ~10-12% occupancy; tm2 register-bound at 255 regs/thread.
- Secondary: four separate tm1 GEMMs (N=128 is a tiny GEMM N-tile → low SM util) and the low-occupancy einsum/tm2.
- Gap to cuequiv: cute 1.732 vs 1.346 ms (L=1024) = ~0.39 ms. cuequiv fuses the gate into the projection GEMM epilogue and avoids the standalone pointwise; the 468 us tm1 pointwise alone exceeds the gap.

## 5. Plan (one focused experiment) — lands in v2
- Hypothesis: fuse the tm1 gate `sigmoid(gate)*proj` from 2 torch kernels (+temp alloc) into ONE elementwise kernel; cuts that stage's memory traffic ~half.
- Expected: tm1 pointwise 468 -> ~234 us; full L=1024 1.73 -> ~1.5 ms.
- v1 is the baseline; experiment is v2.
