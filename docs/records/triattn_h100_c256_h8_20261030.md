# TriangleAttention on H100: d_pair 256 / 8 heads x 32, inference (2026-10-30)

Protenix-v2's trunk (and confidence head) TriangleAttention: d_pair 256, 8 heads x 32, the width-generic path
(`h100_sm90_generic.py`, fp16 inference core `triattn_h16_sm90`). H100 80GB HBM3 (700 W cap), torch 2.13 + cu129, B = 1, no mask,
random weights (std 1/sqrt(fan-in), LN params N(1, .05) / N(0, .05)), `x ~ N(0, 1)`. Module timings are warm CUDA-graph replays
(median of 5 rounds after 0.4 s of replays). "main" is 41345e19 and "new" is this branch, both measured in the same job,
interleaved (2 rounds each, averaged). The Anthropic kit row is `block:triattn_native` in its cu130 container (protenix_v2 kit venv,
the same harness and inputs, job 39443).

## What changed

1. **Core task order by head group** (`triattn_h16_sm90.cu`, host side). The CTAs running together worked on every head of one
   row pair (ORD0) below L 2048. At 8 heads x L1536 the bias of all heads is 37.7 MB, more than the L2 holds next to K / V: ncu showed
   32.5 GB of DRAM reads per call (6 GB needed), DRAM 65 %, L2 79 %, hit rate 49 %. Now the order is query tile, head in its group, row
   group, head group, with the largest group whose bias fits `TA_BIAS_L2` (20 MiB). Below 2048 that keeps all heads for 4 heads x 1536,
   and for 8 heads up to L ~1150. From L 2048 on it stays one head, as before. Sustained core (5 s per variant at the power cap):
   L1536 15.16 -> 13.82 ms. Core A/B (12 rounds):

   | L | ORD0 (all 8) | 1 head | 2 heads | 4 heads |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 768 | 1.785 | 1.790 | 1.818 | 1.811 |
   | 1024 | 4.005 | 4.014 | 4.101 | 4.002 |
   | 1536 | 14.163 | 14.028 | 13.082 | **12.960** |
   | 2048 | 36.25 (4 heads) | **30.02** | 31.06 | 36.25 |

2. **`front_c256`** (`triattn_generic_sm90.cu`) does LN, q | k | v | g and the head-major scaled / masked bias in one kernel, for
   fp16 inference at d_pair 256 x 8 heads, L > 256. A tile is 128 rows. LN runs in registers on the wgmma A fragments (fp16 y, the
   value `ln_fwd_k` writes). The fp16 weights (512 KiB) stream through a 4-stage ring of 64-column chunks shared by both consumer
   warpgroups, with 16 RS m64n64k16 per chunk, double-buffered. Weight rows are permuted per chunk so each lane stores 16
   contiguous bytes, with no staging. The bias leaves through a [8][128] shared tile. It replaces LN, the cuBLAS projection GEMM (and
   with it the y round trip), the bias GEMM and front_bias. Front alone, CUDA graph:

   | L | node | LN + GEMMs + front_bias | ln_bias_fwd + GEMM | front_c256 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 512 | starting | 0.442 | 0.418 | **0.336** |
   | 512 | ending | 0.463 | 0.437 | **0.355** |
   | 1536 | starting | 3.928 | 3.698 | **2.997** |
   | 1536 | ending | 3.961 | 3.857 | **3.193** |

   q | k | v | g against the unfused fp16 front: rel 1.0e-5. sbias is within 1 fp16 ulp, with the same -inf pattern. Bitwise
   deterministic for both nodes, masked or not.

3. **`TA_POLY 8`** (`triattn_h16_sm90.cu`) computes every other exponential pair (f16x2) as a degree-3 polynomial on the FMA
   pipe. `ex2.approx.f16x2` compiles to two `MUFU.EX2.F16`, and the MUFU (XU) ran at 65 %. Sustained core: L512 0.620 -> 0.619,
   768 1.902 -> 1.891, 1024 4.292 -> 4.270, 1536 13.82 -> 13.44, 2048 32.10 -> 31.05 ms. Accuracy against fp32 (real front operands
   and x36 / +20 / +60 log2 shifted logits) was unchanged to 3 digits.

4. `ln_bias_fwd` (LN + bias projection + head-major bias, bulk-copied 128-row blocks) serves d_pair 256 with other head layouts
   (for example 4 x 32): 0.95-0.97x the module time. The fp16 copy of the packed weights is cached on the pack (a cast kernel ran
   every call before).

## Accuracy

Module against an fp32 PyTorch reference, update relative error (out - x): unchanged by every step to 4 digits (L512 / 768, both nodes,
mask on / off; e.g. 2.205e-2 vs 2.205e-2 at L512 starting, which is the bf16 output rounding of the residual stream). Every output is
bitwise deterministic run to run.

## What bounds it next

The core takes ~75 % of the module at L >= 1024. At L1536 (ncu, ORD0) it issues on 39 % of cycles with the XU at 65 % and the tensor
pipe at 34 %. Consumer-loop stall samples sit at the MUFU (fixed-latency waits on the HFMA2 feeding it, and MIO throttle) and at the
bias ldmatrix -> stage release (MEMBAR.ALL.CTA in `fence.proxy.async`). The module runs at the 700 W cap (~1.59 GHz sustained). The
front is now at ~2 TB/s of HBM traffic for 6 GB at L1536 (the floor is ~2.0 ms vs 2.94 ms). The out-projection (cuBLAS, residual in
the epilogue) is at its HBM floor.

Not done: training (the bf16 core and backward at 8 heads keep ORD0 below L 2048; training crops of <= 1024 tokens fit the L2 anyway).
