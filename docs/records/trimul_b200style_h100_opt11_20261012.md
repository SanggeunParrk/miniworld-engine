# TriMul H100 opt11 — overlap the B7 gate tail, 2026-10-12

The selected D64 B7 pipeline is installed behind the existing `MINIWORLD_TRIMUL_H100_B200STYLE=1` path. Its default remains zero. This is a small improvement over the [opt8](trimul_b200style_h100_opt8_20261012.md) native implementation, with two shapes retaining their previous B7 kernel. Fresh installed FP32-master training measurements beat cuEquivariance 0.12.0 in **24/24 D64 shapes, by 2.002738–3.066852x** complete forward+backward. **SOL90 and the requested 70–80% utilization range remain unmet.**

This report does not refresh inference or replace the frozen wider-width [matched-master training audit](trimul_h100_master_matched_20261012.md).

## Implementation and selection

B7 previously completed the projection-input and projection-weight GEMMs, waited for them, then issued and waited for the separate gate-input GEMM. The candidate issues the gate-input GEMM after the final projection-input accumulation and before the final projection-weight GEMM, using their existing final WGMMA commit/wait. Projection-then-gate accumulation order, saved tensors, partial reductions, HBM traffic and B200 fusion boundaries are preserved. No additional activation tensor or kernel is introduced.

New sources are `d64_bwd_wg_gatepipe.cu` and `uni_bwd_wg_gatepipe.cu`; the original `_b7gate.cu` sources remain available. Their host builders select the new `_b2w_gatepipe` symbols for 22 of the 24 D64 shapes in each master-weight regime. Bidirectional L128 and outgoing L256 retain `_b2w_b7gate`. B1 and final reduction symbols remain unchanged. The integration selector and flag-off paths are unchanged.

The two exceptions are based on complete-workload measurements. Bidirectional L128 lost in the first pilot in both master regimes. Outgoing L256 later lost by about 0.14–0.18% in the first selection repeat. The first packaging guard rejected that selection; its failure is retained. The final selector preserves both old kernels. These exceptions concern scheduling within the same fusion, not a departure from B200 fusion.

## Paired native measurements

All measurements use the official runner, one allocated H100, compilation and manual CUDA Graphs, batch 1, one layer, D64, dropout 0.25 and mask probability 0.1. Both master policies are explicit. Each cell uses 90 alternating CUDA-event pairs and exact input/parameter state checks. Actual graph profiles verify the intended B7 symbol and absence of Triton. Independent timed dropout draws are not claimed identical.

There are **126 paired cells** including the retained first-selection repeat of 18 cells. The largest individual ratio is not a typical or guaranteed gain. Some differences are near measurement noise: unchanged control cells themselves fluctuate by roughly 0.55%. The full-grid improvement of the selected cells is about **0.02–1.32%**, and the two capture-order repeats support the final selection without establishing a confidence interval.

Pilot/full-grid jobs are 28205/28210, first selection 28214, final selection 28229, and reversed capture order 28235. Candidate CUDA bytes are identical across the selector repeats.

B7 has zero stack/spill bytes and zero local-memory sectors. B1 is unchanged and retains its prior bidirectional 8-byte spill. DRAM bytes remain essentially unchanged. Short-scoreboard and memory dependencies remain; the data does not establish a hard fusion limit.
