# TriMul H100 opt15 — D384 backward K1 gradient prefetch, 2026-10-12

D384 training now overlaps contraction-gradient TMA reads with front-projection recomputation. Relative to [opt14](trimul_b200style_h100_opt14_20261012.md), **92/92 resident complete F+B comparisons improve by 1.009895–1.034748x**, with a **1.019619x geometric mean**. The largest bidirectional condition also improves in all four separate ABBA/BAAB cohorts. Fresh FP32-master training comparisons beat cuEquivariance 0.12.0 in **24/24 shapes, by 1.374422–2.758386x**, geometric mean **2.276822x**.

The change is installed behind the existing `MINIWORLD_TRIMUL_H100_B200STYLE=1` path; its default remains zero. Inference and other widths retain their algorithms. **SOL90 remains unmet.** The synchronization-check scope and remaining limitation are stated below.

## Implementation and selection

The original RS K1 issues its dplane TMA load after the preactivation MMAs retire and immediately waits for the data. The new path issues that same load at the beginning of the MMA iteration. The previous TMA store-read completion wait, proxy fence and warpgroup barrier still protect the staging buffer before the new load. The load-completion mbarrier, transpose WAR barrier and final store-completion wait remain in place.

No tensor, allocation, kernel launch, shared-memory buffer or numerical operation is added or removed. B200 fusion boundaries, front recomputation, reductions, BF16 rounding, saved layouts and the existing geometry are retained: 128 tokens, two consumer warpgroups, two six-chunk weight slots, schedule 1. This is an implementation change within the B200 algorithm.

Eleven feasible RS geometries/schedules are measured through the official complete-workload runner. Three additional declarations are rejected statically because their rings cannot hold a full weight block before release, which would deadlock the producer and consumer. The best measured geometry-only geometric mean is 1.002394x across two pilot conditions, so no geometry change is selected. Four prefetch configurations each pass six independent FP32-oracle gradient fixtures before their eight total outgoing/bidirectional L384 pilot comparisons. The original geometry with prefetch gives 1.016955x outgoing and 1.022611x bidirectional and is selected.

Three files implement the installed change: the D384-backward selector in `h100_b200style.py`, the header transformation in `h100_b200style_k1_prefetch.py`, and the existing GPU test's K1 dispatch assertion. The generated-header cache key includes the transformation, base headers and epilogue bytes. Opaque operation names distinguish the frozen baseline and candidate. Packaging uses a stable kernel entry name; both H384 and H768 have **identical GPU instruction encodings** to the measured candidate. Both baseline and candidate report **168 registers, zero stack/local storage and zero local load/store instructions**.

## Correctness and synchronization

The experimental candidate and final packaged source each pass **73 GPU tests**. Across all 48 D384 conditions, forward output and eight saved tensors match opt14 bitwise, and every input/parameter gradient meets the unchanged independent FP32 oracle: relative Frobenius error below 0.006 for output and 0.01 for each gradient. Maximum native errors in the resident grid are **0.003603762 / 0.006386934**. Gradient bitwise equality is not required.

Eight standalone K1 cases cover H384/H768 at L128/256/384/768. Each changes input, packed weights, affines, mask and incoming gradients over three graph replays, including zero masks; packed output gradients match the old K1 bitwise. Six full-module L256 graphs cover inference and training in all directions while changing inputs, parameters, masks, dropout scales and dy.

Unfiltered standalone K1 **memcheck, synccheck and racecheck** pass H384/H768 at L128/256, including persistent-buffer reuse. Unfiltered full-module memcheck passes all six changed-value cases. Full-module synccheck and racecheck restricted to the changed K1 pass the three training cases; the inference cases do not launch that kernel. All reported checks have zero errors/hazards/warnings. Full-module synccheck with WGMMA checking disabled also passes all six cases; it does not substitute for WGMMA validation. The packaged kernel additionally passes unfiltered standalone synccheck with explicit verification that the untouched output tail retains its initial bytes.

**Unfiltered whole-module synccheck remains incomplete.** This turn's frozen opt14 outgoing-training control times out after 75 seconds. The packaged opt15 full control times out after 120 seconds at outgoing training before capture completes, after its bidirectional inference/training and outgoing-inference changed-value cases finish. Focused custom-only and external-only controls pass separately. A specific kernel cause or instrumentation defect has not been established. Neither timeout is counted as a pass; the change-scoped and standalone checks above do not claim unfiltered whole-module coverage.

**Correction to opt14:** its diagnostic prose calls numeric `direction=1` incoming. The kernel and reference define **0=bidirectional, 1=outgoing, 2=incoming**. The stalled condition in that earlier diagnostic was outgoing training. Raw logs and the original dated record remain unchanged. Official named-direction benchmark rows are unaffected.

Two tooling failures are retained: an initial observer reused its K1 tuple variable for an OmegaConf object, and the first binary-inspection script omitted an explicit CUDA device index. Both are corrected before their successful reruns; neither failed attempt contributes a successful measurement.
