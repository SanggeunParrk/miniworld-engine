## What the engine spent its time on (profiled, L384)
Inference: pair LayerNorm over N^2 rows of 16 (782 µs) + the 16 -> 4 projection as an sm80 cutlass GEMM (1193 µs); attention 200 µs.
Training: pair LayerNorm backward 5466 µs, attention backward 4283 + 586 µs, forward 1468 µs, pair GEMMs 2355 µs.

## The kernels
- **pair_bias.cu**: LN(z) . Wb^T with gamma folded into the projection, one memory-bound pass writing the bias head-major and transposed
  (for dK / dV); the backward gives dz, dgamma, dWb from dbias. N = 3072: 97.5 µs fwd (4.6 TB/s), 228 µs bwd (engine ~2.0 / 7.9 ms).
- **attn_fwd.cu**: the token-DiT B200 forward at 4 x 32 (odd A handled: the spare warpgroup computes a duplicate and does not store).
- **backward in three passes** (a design change from the token DiT, forced by N): with the token split (dQ + dbias in one pass) the dbias
  sum over samples needs either ~2 GB of L2 atomics (H100 design) or ~1.8 GB of dQ partials (token B200 design, one per 128-key chunk,
  N / 128 = 24 at N = 3072). Instead: attn_dq (sample, head, query tile; dQ resident in TMEM, one TMA store per item), attn_dkv (token
  kernel at atom shapes), attn_dbias (head, query tile, key chunk; all samples; dbias in registers). No atomics, no partial sums; the
  price is one more exponential per element.
