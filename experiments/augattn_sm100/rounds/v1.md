## Forward kernel
- Layout facts (n48_test.cu): N = 48 with an MN-major B that fills 48 of a 64-element 128-B swizzle atom works; K = 48 inside one atom
  (3 K-steps) works — v can be used as loaded ([keys][48], 128-B pitch), no transpose.
- v1 (attn_fwd.cu): CTA = (head, 128-query tile, sample pair); two softmax warpgroups (one per sample) share the bias tile in shared
  memory; S = QK^T (M128 N128 K48) and O += PV (TS, M128 N48) through TMEM. 101 / 291 µs kernel. The host-side bias prep (x log2 e,
  bf16) cost 16 / 47 µs: folded into the kernel (t = s / sqrt 48 + bias in natural units, one FFMA; the exponent's FFMA carries
  log2 e) — also more accurate (O rel 1.5e-3 vs fp64, LSE 1.7e-6; the sm_90a kernel 4.7e-3).
- v2 (attn_fwd2.cu): persistent CTAs, 64-key blocks, 4 stages (48 KB), double-buffered S, one MMA warp per softmax warpgroup,
  fp32x2 softmax: **87.1-88 µs (L384) / 272-277 µs (L768)**.

## Where it stands (ncu, trace)
Softmax per 64-key block ~1.8 k clk for two warpgroups on shared SMSPs vs a MUFU floor of 1.0 k: issue 34 %, tensor 15 %, L2 26 % —
latency-bound with two softmax warps per SMSP. Tried: polynomial exp2 on the FMA pipe for 25-75 % of the exponentials (slower:
not MUFU-bound), split max / sum chains (no change), 64-wide TMA boxes (no change). The next lever is more softmax warps per SMSP
(4 samples per CTA with 32-key blocks) — a rewrite; deferred behind the backward, which is the larger share of the training time.
