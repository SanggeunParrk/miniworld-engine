## The backward (same fusion as the H100 pair: attn_dqb = dQ + dbias, attn_dkv = dK + dV)
- **attn_dqb.cu**: a CTA owns (head, 128 queries, 128-key chunk) and walks ALL 48 samples; TMEM holds S, dP, dS and dQ, so
  the dbias tile of the chunk lives in registers and is written once with plain stores — no zero fill, no atomics (the
  H100 kernel summed 3 samples on chip and pushed the rest to L2 with atomics). dQ becomes partial per key chunk and leaves
  through TMA bulk reduce-adds (cp.reduce.async.bulk.tensor, smem-staged) into a dQ that attn_dkv zero-fills on the way.
- **attn_dkv.cu**: transposed as on H100 (one key row per thread); two warpgroups take alternate query blocks and feed one
  dK / dV accumulator pair through two MMA warps (every MMA accumulates; the epilogue zeroes after the read-out); the bias
  arrives transposed (Triton tile transpose), LSE / D by bulk copy.

## What moved the numbers (and what did not)
| change | L384 | L768 |
|---|---:|---:|
| dqb first version (2 samples of S in flight, red.v4 dQ) | 150 | 498 |
| dqb: sample start rotated by 1 per key chunk (was A / chunks: working set left L2; DRAM read 387 -> 182 MB) | 118 | 448 |
| dqb: dQ by TMA bulk reduce-add instead of red.v4 | 116 | 407 |
| dqb: q / dO / V ring released after S / dP, K ring after dQ; dQ(g) never queued behind S(g + 1) | 95 | 356 |
| dqb: incremental item / sample indices (runtime divisions cost ~900 clk per step) | 87 | 340 |
| dkv: dK / dV through smem-staged TMA stores (per-row st.v4 = 32 lines per warp store: 6-8 k clk bubble per item) | 130 -> 102 | 381 -> 358 |
| forward: same TMA-store epilogue for O + incremental indices | 88 -> 69 | 277 -> 234 |

Measured and rejected: 3-stage ring for dqb v1 (not load-latency bound), head-major input copies (no change: the
misaligned 96-B rows are not the cost), four dS warpgroups in dkv (attn_dkv4.cu: 154 / 463, slower), software-pipelined
TMEM loads (no change), two MMA issuers alone (no change without the ordering fix).

Instruments kept: microbenchmarks mufu_bench.cu (ex2 peak 31 / ns / SM = 16 / clk: MUFU is NOT the limit anywhere yet) and
mma_lat.cu (tcgen05 round trip 340 clk; S + dP 523 clk), trace_dqb.py / trace_dkv.py (clock64 timelines), energy.py (NVML
energy per call), prof_bwd.py (ncu via gcsudo).

## Where it stands
dqb per step ~3 k clk vs a 1 k clk MUFU floor: the epilogue (tcgen05 read, zero, smem staging, 2 named barriers, reduce issue)
is ~1 k clk and 0.6 k clk remain unexplained between steps; the remaining dQ reduction costs ~30 / 77 µs. dkv at L768 runs at
the 1000 W cap. The glue (27 / 51 µs) is mostly prep_do at DRAM bandwidth (reads fp32 dO and O).
