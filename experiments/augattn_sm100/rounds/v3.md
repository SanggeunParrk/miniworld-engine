# augattn_sm100 — round v3: FA4-style exponential ping-pong (PP) in all four cores

- Date: 2026-09-29. A = 48, bf16 operands, fp32 sums; CUDA-graph replay on GPU 6 through `gpuq`, nothing else on the GPU
  (1965 MHz at start of each step). Logs: `../token_dit_fused/results/b200/2026-09-29_{nopp,pp}/log.txt`.

## Why
The source-line profile of attn_fwd2 (`srcprof.py`: ncu SASS samples per source line) put 25.5 % of warp samples on ex2
(mio throttle, MUFU queue full) while MUFU averaged 48 %: the two softmax warpgroups hit their exp phase together.

## Change (d5577279, measured here)
PP = 1: the two softmax (fwd2, inf) / dS (dqb, dkv) warpgroups take turns on the exponentials through named barriers 3 / 4
(warpgroup 1 pre-arrives so warpgroup 0 goes first); the other warpgroup's S load, bias, max and P store run under it.
PMOD / PCNT move a share of exp2 onto the FMA pipe as a polynomial.
