## qkvg_fwd.cu (the port of swa_qkvg_fwd.cu)
Persistent CTAs, weights resident in smem, tiles of SP augments x AT atoms (16 x 8 training, 5 x 25 inference), x -> TMEM as the A
operand, four M128 N128 K128 TS MMAs into two alternating accumulators, one row per thread, two warpgroups splitting x channels and heads.
Same rounding points as the Triton kernel: 99.97 % of outputs bitwise equal, the rest 1 ulp (accumulation order).
