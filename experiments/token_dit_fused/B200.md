# Token DiT on B200 (sm_100a): inference step and training block

Status 2026-09-29. Box: NVIDIA B200 (khidi_hjju, 1000 W power cap), torch 2.10.0+cu130, GPU 6 through `gpuq`, nothing
else on the GPU during any measurement (logged per step in `results/b200/*/log.txt`). The H100 story of this package is
in `README.md`; this file covers what changed for B200 and where it stands.

Two paths, both bf16 GEMM operands with fp32 accumulation and an fp32 residual stream:

- **Inference step**: the H100 v7 schedule (`tdit/runner.py`, `FusedTokenDiT.step`) with the attention core swapped for the
  sm_100a kernel `augattn_sm100/src/attn_inf.cu` (`tdit/b200_core.py`). The runner picks it on capability 10.0.
- **Training block** (forward + backward): `tdit/train_b200.py`, new for B200. One autograd Function per block, the
  pair bias hoisted for the whole stack, the sm_100a training core (`attn_fwd2` + `attn_dqb` + `attn_dkv`).

## What changed on 2026-09-29: FA4-style exponential ping-pong (PP)

The two softmax (forward) / dS (backward) warpgroups of every core now take turns on the exponentials through named
barriers, so one warpgroup's S load, bias add, max and P store run under the other's MUFU phase. The source-line profile
of `attn_fwd2` had shown 25.5 % of warp samples stalled on ex2 while MUFU averaged 48 %: both warpgroups reached the
exp phase together. Sweep (`augattn_sm100/sweep_fa4.sh`, `t_core.py`, us):

| kernel | L | PP = 0 | **PP** | PP + 1/4 poly exp | PP + 2/4 poly | PP + 3/8 poly |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| attn_fwd2 | 384 | 69.2 | **65.9** | 67.7 | 70.5 | 68.3 |
| attn_dqb | 384 | 95.4 | **93.4** | 100.3 | 105.7 | 103.8 |
| attn_dkv | 384 | 100.8 | **99.7** | 102.1 | 106.7 | 103.1 |
| attn_fwd2 | 768 | 234.1 | **210.1** | 217.2 | 232.8 | 221.5 |
| attn_dqb | 768 | 342.5 | **332.0** | 353.6 | 375.1 | 366.3 |
| attn_dkv | 768 | 343.5 | **337.4** | 348.2 | 366.3 | 352.6 |

PP is bit-identical to PP = 0 in the forward and within 2e-7 in the backward. A polynomial exp2 on the FMA pipe loses
everywhere once PP is on (and adds 4-6e-4 error): the cores are not MUFU-throughput bound. PP = 1 is the source default;
the cubins before it are kept in `augattn_sm100/build/v2_nopp/` on the box.

## Training block: structure (`tdit/train_b200.py`)

Same math as `modules.dit.DiTBlock` with qk_norm (AF3 Alg. 23 + 25), rearranged:

- **Pair bias for the whole stack** (`_PairBiasAll`): all blocks' `ln_pair` share the LayerNorm statistics of a pair row,
  so each block's LN weight folds into its 128 -> 16 projection and every block's bias is ONE LayerNorm + ONE GEMM,
  head-major bf16. The backward runs once for all blocks: two GEMMs and one LN-backward row kernel; blocks leave their
  dbias in a shared store.
- **Conditioning as two GEMMs**: every block-internal LayerNorm of cond shares its statistics, so its weights fold into
  the projection (`chat @ [Ws1 w1; Wb1 w1; Ws2 w2; Wb2 w2]^T`); the two output gates read raw cond.
- **q|k|v|g as one GEMM**, qk-norm in a row kernel, the sm_100a core on bf16 q, k, v.
- **SwiGLU in the expand GEMM** (quack sm100 `gemm_act`, gate/up rows interleaved); backward `gemm_dact` computes
  dh = dz Wsq^T with the SwiGLU backward in its epilogue and recomputes h for the squeeze wgrad (h is not saved).
- **Every elementwise step is one Triton row kernel** (`tdit/train_kernels.py`): AdaLN, residual + gate + next AdaLN,
  gate on o, and their backwards; bias / qk-norm / gate gradient sums accumulate atomically inside the backward row
  kernels into one zeroed buffer. Row kernel shapes come from `tune_rows.py` (4.5-6.3 TB/s).
- **bf16 weight pack cached**, rebuilt only when a parameter's `_version` changes (optimizer step).

|---|---|---:|---|

| T2 | 565da489 | 2068 | bias reductions fused into backward row kernels, weight-pack cache, TF32 pair-bias dots; 89 -> 45 launches |
| T3 | e25ace6a | 1926 | SwiGLU fused into quack sm100 GEMM epilogues (forward and backward) |

## Inference core notes

- `attn_inf.cu` is `attn_fwd2` adapted to the step: q|k|v|g read as column views of the GEMM output, logits pre-scaled
  into exp2 units, an odd S handled by a clamped partner sample, g loaded by TMA and `sigmoid(g) * o` written over q by a
  TMA store. Core at S = 5: L384 16.1 -> 13.3 us, L768 41.8 -> 35.1 us against the Triton core (before PP).
- Tried and not taken (commit dc8c4aa8): a double-buffered P and `attn_inf2` (one item's keys split over the two
  warpgroups) are flat at S = 5; the core is bound by one item's latency there (S = 1: 12.4 / 8.5 us).

## Reproduce

On the box, with the engine source of this branch at `$W/mw-dit/src` and the old uv venv (`$W/miniworld-engine/.venv`,
torch 2.10), which every number here uses:

    cd $W/mw-dit/experiments/token_dit_fused
    $W/gpuq/gpuq run -g 6 -n tdit -- bash measure_b200.sh        # [OUT=dir] -- log.txt + step JSONs

`measure_b200.sh` builds `attn_fwd2`, `attn_dqb`, `attn_dkv` and `attn_inf` (PP = 1 by default), sets the environment of
`run_b200.sh`, checks before every step that nothing else is on the GPU, and runs `bench_train.py L`, `bench.py --length
L --save ...` and `train_block.py --length L --stack 4 --variants pytorch engine-b200 tdit-b200` for L = 384 and 768,
one process each. `augattn_sm100/sweep_fa4.sh 384 768` re-runs the PP sweep.

## Profile of the training block and what was tried after PP (2026-09-29)

Per-kernel CUDA time (`train_block.py --stack 4 --profile`), L384, per block of ~1570 us:

| group | us | share | note |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| GEMMs (cuBLAS nvjet, quack gated fwd / bwd, split-K reduce) | ~738 | 47 % | 913 GFLOP at ~1.24 PFLOP/s: at the power-capped cuBLAS ceiling (~1.30 PF) |
| Triton row kernels (AdaLN, residual + gate, qk-norm, cond, and backwards) | ~480 | 31 % | each within ~65-85 % of 7 TB/s |
| attention cores (fwd2, dqb, dkv) | ~267 | 17 % | 30 % at L768 (1072 of ~3500 us) |

The max per-tensor gradient error is not one outlier: many parameters sit at 8.6-8.7e-3 at L384 (ln_pair.weight,
to_scale.weight, to_query.bias, ...) and 9.5-10e-3 at L768 (norm_query / norm_key weights) -- the bf16-operand floor.
Two repeated runs gave 8.74e-3 exactly; the 1.46e-2 of the PP = 0 run above was not reproduced.

Tried:

| change | result |
|---|---|
| `_unfold_lnw`: one program per 16 rows of the 3072 x 384 dWn, column sum then one atomic (was one program per row: 3072 programs x 384 atomics onto 768 addresses) | **kept**: 22-26 us -> below the top-25 kernels (< ~11 us) per block |
| cond LayerNorm + bf16 copy (`_cond_prep`) once per stack: every block reads the same cond | **kept**: one launch per stack instead of per block, ~13 / ~25 us per block at L384 / L768 |
| backward row kernels walking NIT row chunks per program, atomics once at the end (~600 programs) | **reverted**: slower (`_res_adaln_b_bwd` 83 -> 113 us, `_adaln_a_bwd` 61 -> 74 us): the atomics were not the cost, the lost parallelism was |
| cores read v in place from q\|k\|v\|g (strided TMA rows), `_qknorm` stops copying v | **reverted**: `_qknorm` -6.6 / -10 us per block, but the cores looked ~1-2 % slower at L768 relative to unchanged GEMMs in the same run; net not resolvable (below) |

**Measurement noise on this card.** Under the 1000 W cap the same code's kernel-time total moves by +-4 % between runs a
few seconds apart (L768: 13983-15177 us; `attn_dkv` alone 1545-1725 us), and the eager wall time by +-3-5 %. Changes of
1-3 % are not resolvable from a few end-to-end runs: compare per-kernel times, normalise by an unchanged kernel of the
same run, or measure energy per call (`augattn_sm100/energy.py`).

## Where it can go next

- The row kernels are near their byte floor; what is left there is fusion (fewer bytes), e.g. the attention forward
  writing sigmoid(g) o directly (as `attn_inf` does) instead of O + `_gate_o`, if D = rowsum(dO o) can come from og
  without losing accuracy.
- The GEMMs are at the power-capped ceiling: only fewer FLOPs or fewer bytes help.
- The attention cores are the largest gap to their own ceiling (30 % of the block at L768); see
  `augattn_sm100/rounds/v2.md` for what has and has not worked there.
- Not done: key mask in the training path (the inference path folds it into the hoisted bias), QK-norm in the
  inference path.
