## 3. What was built

The fusion is the sm_90a one (`experiments/transition_fused/src/transition_{fwd,bwd}.cu`), unchanged in what is fused, what is
saved, where values are rounded, and in which order the hidden chunks accumulate. Blackwell has no register-operand MMA, so the
mapping to hardware is new:

**Forward** (`src/tfwd.cu`, 384 threads, 1 CTA/SM persistent over 128-row tiles): warp 0 TMA x tiles, warp 3 TMA weight chunks
through a 2-slot ring, warp 1 issues tcgen05.mma, warps 4-7 SwiGLU, warps 8-11 LayerNorm of the next tile + residual epilogue.
`[a|b]` (M128 N128 K128, SS) lands in one of two TMEM buffers; the SwiGLU warps read it one row per thread, form
`h = bf16(silu(a) b)` with the kit sigmoid and write `h` back to TMEM; the squeeze is an A-from-TMEM product (TS, M128 N128 K64)
accumulating across all eight chunks in a third TMEM region — the Blackwell analogue of the H100 register-source squeeze. `h` never
touches shared or global memory. LayerNorm is one row per thread and reproduces the sm_90a reduction tree exactly (32 partials of
four columns, xor-tree 16/8/4/2/1), so `xn` / `rstd` / `c1` are bit-comparable to the H100 kernel. `xn` is saved by TMA store
from the operand tile; inference skips it at run time (`save = 0`).

**Backward** (`src/tbwd.cu`, same two roles as H100): 8 × R DW CTAs keep their slice's Ws / Wa / Wb resident, recompute dh and
`[a|b]`, write `h`, `dA`, `dB` to shared memory and accumulate `[dWa_s; dWb_s]` (M128 N128) and `dWs_s` (M128 N64) in TMEM with
MN-major operands. The remaining DX CTAs stream the eight weight chunks per tile: `dh_j` and `[a|b]_j` into TMEM, the gate writes
`[dA|dB]` back over the `[a|b]` buffer as bf16, `d_xn` accumulates with an A-from-TMEM product over the same packed
`[Wa_j; Wb_j]` tile (MN-major B). The LayerNorm backward reads `d_xn` from TMEM one row per thread; dgamma / dbeta are reduced
with a warp reduce-scatter and kept per lane across tiles. No atomics; a small kernel sums the partials.

## 4. Validation (L128 / L384 / L768)
- Forward vs the contract emulated in torch (same rounding points): out rel 8.6e-5 (0.12 % of elements differ by one bf16 ulp —
  approximate vs exact sigmoid), xn 1.3e-5, rstd / c1 2.5e-8. vs fp32: 2.25e-3, identical to the contract's own 2.27e-3.
- Backward vs the contract: dx 1.6e-4, dWa / dWb / dWs 4.2e-4 (L384), dgamma / dbeta 1.4e-4. vs fp32 autograd every gradient is
  as close as the contract itself (dx 3.10e-3 vs 3.10e-3, dWa 3.9e-3 vs 3.5e-3 before bf16 output rounding).
- Bit-reproducible run to run (fwd and all six gradients).

## 5. SoL accounting
Ceilings measured on this card (`profiles/v1/`):
- Tensor: tcgen05.mma M128 N128 K16 chains, one CTA per SM, **8188 FLOP/clk/SM** (SS and TS alike) = 2.38 PFLOP/s at 1965 MHz.
  N = 64 only reaches 5458 FLOP/clk (67 %). cuBLAS 8192³ reaches 1.58 PFLOP/s, so it is not used as the ceiling.
- HBM: read 6.6 TB/s, write 7.6 TB/s, read+write streams ≈ 6.9–7.0 TB/s (spec 8 TB/s).
- MUFU: 16 op/clk/SM = 4.65 Top/s.

| op, L384 | tensor floor | HBM floor | MUFU floor (kit sigmoid, 2/elem) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| forward, 6·M·D·H = 58.0 GFLOP | **24.3 µs** | 10.8 µs (inference) / 16.4 µs (with saves) | 32.4 µs |
| backward, design 22·M·D·H = 212.6 GFLOP | **89.3 µs** (min 16·M·D·H: 64.9 µs) | 21.6 µs | 64.9 µs |

SoL = the tensor floor (the strict one; the MUFU floor is higher than it for the forward, so reaching 90 % will require moving part
of the exponential off the MUFU pipe). L768 floors are 4× these.

## 7. Bottleneck theory (forward, from the clock64 trace, `profiles/v1/trace_fwd.txt`)
- A tile takes ~20 k clk = 8 chunks × **2222 clk**; the tensor work per chunk is 768 clk (expand 512 + squeeze 256, measured
  rates), so the tensor pipe idles ~65 % of the time. Classification: **latency-bound on the chunk pipeline**.
- The SwiGLU warp per chunk: waits 416 clk for `[a|b]`, 1341 clk TMEM load + math, 250 clk h store. Its MUFU work alone is
  1024 clk/chunk, so it runs at ~75 % MUFU utilisation *while busy* but stalls on the expand.
- Why the expand is late: the weight ring has two 48 KB slots holding `[Wa_j; Wb_j]` **and** `Ws_j`. Slot (c+1) is refilled only
  after squeeze(c-1) completes, so the L2→smem TMA latency of the next chunk sits on the critical path every chunk. The MMA issue
  period equals the SwiGLU period (2223 vs 2222 clk), i.e. the two are in lockstep through that dependency.
- Secondary: one SwiGLU warp per SMSP cannot hide TMEM-load and MUFU latency (1341 clk for 1024 clk of MUFU issue).

## 8. Next ideas (ranked)
1. Split the weight ring: `[Wa_j; Wb_j]` freed as soon as expand(j) completes, `Ws_j` freed after squeeze(j) — the refill of the next
   expand's operand then starts ~one chunk earlier and leaves the critical path.
2. Two SwiGLU warpgroups (ping-pong on the two `[a|b]` buffers) to hide TMEM/MUFU latency; setmaxnreg to fund the registers.
3. Backward: same ring split for the DX role, merge the N = 64 dh product with `[a|b]` into one N = 192 accumulator, DW/DX balance.
4. Move a fraction of `ex2` onto the FMA pipe (polynomial) once MUFU becomes the binding unit.
