# transition_fused_sm100 — round v12: energy attribution — why SoL90 is out of reach on this card

- Date: 2026-09-25 (after the B200's other tenant released the GPUs at 21:20)
- Kernels unchanged (v8 forward default in `bench_all.py`, v9 backward).

## 1. Energy attribution of the forward (`energy_abl.py`, NVML energy, ~3 s sustained, inference L384; `profiles/v12/energy_abl.txt`)

| build | µs / call | mJ / call | pJ / FLOP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| forward (v6 1-CTA build) | 63.9 | 62.90 | 1.085 |
| − SwiGLU math (`-DABL_NOMATH`) | 64.3 | 63.35 | 1.093 |
| − LayerNorm work (`-DABL_NOLN`) | 59.5 | 58.62 | 1.011 |
| − SwiGLU TMEM ld/st (`-DABL_NOSWLD`) | 59.3 | 58.64 | 1.011 |
| − weight reloads (`-DABL_NOW`) | 59.8 | 58.41 | 1.007 |
| − all four (MMAs, epilogue, x/out I/O only) | **46.7** | 45.86 | 0.791 |

Every build draws ~980-988 W, so time = energy. The two MUFU ops per hidden element cost essentially nothing; the non-tensor energy
is data movement — LayerNorm, the `[a|b]`/h TMEM round trip and the weight streaming, ~7 % each. With all of it removed the kernel
reaches 0.79 pJ/FLOP, the dense cuBLAS GEMM's own level (0.75).

## 2. Consequence for the target
SoL90 at L384 forward against the 1.58 PFLOP/s ceiling is 40.8 µs. The skeleton above — which skips LayerNorm, the SwiGLU and all
but the first weight chunk, i.e. computes nothing correct — runs 46.7 µs (79 % of that SoL). No correct kernel doing the same MMAs on
this card can be faster than its own skeleton, so **SoL90 (1.58 PF basis) is not attainable here for this op**, independent of
further scheduling work. Against the sustained dense-GEMM ceiling (1.30 PF, 44.6 µs), SoL90 would be 49.6 µs; the correct forward is
at 63.9 µs sustained (70 %), the skeleton at 95 %.

## 3. Also measured
`__nanosleep` back-off inside every mbarrier wait (backward, 64 / 256 ns): no energy or time change — `mbarrier.try_wait` already
parks waiting warps.
