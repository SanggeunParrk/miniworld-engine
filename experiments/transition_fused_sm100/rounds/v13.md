# transition_fused_sm100 — round v13: backward skeleton — the training-step SoL90 is out of reach too

- Date: 2026-09-25
- Kernels unchanged (v9 backward). New ablation switches in `src/tbwd.cu` (off by default): `-DABL_BGATE` (DW and DX gates skip
  their TMEM loads, math and stores and only signal), `-DABL_BEPI` (the DX LayerNorm-backward epilogue skips its TMEM loads and
  math and only signals). Script `energy_babl.py`; numbers in `profiles/v13/energy_babl.txt`.

The gates (TMEM round trips of dh / `[a|b]` / `[dA|dB]` and the h / dA / dB shared-memory stores in DW) are ~22 % of the backward;
the epilogue alone hides behind the chunk pipeline. With both removed — computing nothing correct — the backward still needs
175.8 µs.

## 2. Consequence
Backward SoL90 on the 1.58 PFLOP/s basis (design 22·M·D·H) is 149.5 µs; the skeleton is 176 µs (76 %). Training-step SoL90 is
190 µs; the two skeletons alone sum to ~47 + 176 = 223 µs. With v12 this closes the question on this card: under its 1000 W cap
no correct implementation of this fusion reaches SoL90 against the 1.58 PFLOP/s basis, and the remaining gap of the correct kernels
to their skeletons is 27 % (forward, 63.9 vs 46.7 µs sustained) and 33 % (backward, 234 vs 176 µs).

## 3. Addendum — sustained step vs R, and DH_PAIR (not adopted)
Sustained (~3 s, NVML) training step, v8 forward + v9 backward (`sustained_step.py`): L384 R 8/9/10 = 323.2 / **318.4** / 328.5 µs,
L768 = 1309.2 / **1181.4** / 1236.7 µs — R = 9 stays. Against the sustained dense-GEMM ceiling (1.30 PF) the step is 65 % (L384)
and 71 % (L768) sustained, 71 % / 73 % in the short graph benchmark.
DH_PAIR (`records/experiments/bwd_dh_pair.patch`): DX dh of two chunks as one N = 128 product over the two adjacent Ws slots (N = 64
MN-major runs at 58 % of peak in isolation). 225.8 vs 218.1 µs (R 9): pairing makes dh wait for the gate to have read dh of the two
previous chunks, which costs more than the tensor time saved.
