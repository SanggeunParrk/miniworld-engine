# transition_fused_sm100 — round v14: backward without the weight role's recompute (algorithm change; measured, not adopted)

- Date: 2026-09-25. The user allowed changing the H100 fusion algorithm.
- New kernel `src/tbwd2.cu` (+ `FusedBwd2`, `bench_bwd.py --v2`); the default stays v9 (`tbwd.cu`).

## 1. Design
The DX CTAs already form dA / dB for every (tile, chunk); they now also form h and write the half-rows of [dA | dB | h] of that
(tile, chunk) to a global exchange buffer (48 KB per (tile, chunk)), then raise a per-(tile, chunk) flag (8 gate warps, release).
The DW CTA of that chunk (= hidden slice) polls the flag (acquire), TMA-loads the block, drops its lines from L2 with
`discard.global.L2` (so they are never written back to HBM), resets the flag for the next launch, and runs only the weight-gradient
products. Tensor work 22 → 16 M·D·H; the DW role needs no weights, no dh / `[a|b]` and no gate. Values are bitwise those the DW
role used to recompute.

## 2. Validation
L128: dx 1.6e-4, dWa / dWb / dWs 3.2-3.5e-4, dgamma / dbeta 1.6-1.7e-4 vs the contract; bit-reproducible (flags reset in-kernel).

## 3. Performance (L384 backward, CUDA-graph replay)

| DW replicas R (DW / DX CTAs) | full design | no global stores, no flag waits (lower bound) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 3 (24 / 124) | 505 µs | — |
| 4 (32 / 116) | 395 µs | 332 µs |
| 5 (40 / 108) | 412 µs | 270 µs |
| 6 (48 / 100) | 440 µs | **246 µs** |
| 7 (56 / 92) | 470 µs | 260 µs |

v9: 218-234 µs. Per-role exits (`span_bwd.py R v2`, lower-bound build): DX 222 / 238 / 252 µs at 108 / 100 / 92 CTAs — the DX role's
time barely depends on how many SMs it has (v9: 76 CTAs, 211-225 µs), i.e. it is set by its energy under the 1000 W cap, not by
SM count. The DW role, freed of compute, becomes TMA-intake-bound: each slice-tile needs its 48 KB block plus the shared 64 KB of
dy / xn, ~1 GB of intake per backward (v9: ~0.5 GB), so it still needs ~50 SMs. With the stores and flags on, DX runs ahead of the
slower DW, the exchange data in flight outgrows L2 and spills to HBM, and the design falls to 395-470 µs.

## 4. Conclusion
Removing the recompute trades tensor work for data movement; on this power-capped card the trade loses (lower bound 246 µs vs
218-234 µs). The recompute of the H100 design is the cheaper option here.
