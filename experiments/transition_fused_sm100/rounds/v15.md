## 2. Candidates examined

| candidate | outcome |
|---|---|
| Fold LayerNorm into the weights (expand x, fix up with per-row mean / rstd) | rejected on paper: the fix-up lands on the H = 512 × 2 expand outputs instead of the D = 128 inputs — ~4× the work it removes |
| 4-CTA clusters (quarter the weight reads) on 132 SMs | fewer SMs cost more than the ~3.5 % weight energy: 132 SMs → forward +6 %, backward +13 %; 120 SMs → +12 % / +32 % |
| Exchange [dA \| dB \| h] instead of recomputing (v14) | lower bound 246 µs vs 218-234 |
| Single-gate backward: each slice CTA also forms its d_xn partial from the dA / dB it already stores, partials reduce-scattered over a cluster of 8 (no DX role, 16 M·D·H) | the reduce-scatter alone costs **9014 clk per tile** (`dsmem_bench.cu`, 120 CTAs) vs ~3000 clk for a whole DW tile today — rejected, like the sm_90a cluster variant |

## 3. What remains
The gates' cost is the TMEM traffic of fp32 accumulators (dh, a, b read as fp32 — 12 B per hidden element per role) plus the
[dA | dB] / h write-backs. Within the current precision contract no layout found removes it; halving it needs 16-bit accumulators or
16-bit re-reads, i.e. a precision change.
