## 2. Findings
- Backward is latency-bound, not power-bound, at R = 14: removing the DW dy conversion entirely saves 24 mJ per call (161.9 → 137.4 mJ)
  but no time (165.5 µs, 830 W). Its critical path is the DX role: 8.5 k clk per tile, set by the LayerNorm-backward epilogue
  (pass 1 ≈ 3.4 k, pass 2 + store ≈ 3.8 k clk on two warps per SMSP), which is why R = 15 (DW 140 µs) loses (DX 193 µs).
- Tried, not adopted: shared-memory transpose sums for dgamma / dbeta instead of warp reduce-scatters (`EPI3`, needs NDAB = 2): DX
  10.3 k clk per tile, slower; deferring the dx store-read wait by one tile (`STORE_ASYNC`): no gain; PDL quantize → forward: no gain.
- What would move it further: more warps on the DX epilogue (needs the loader roles packed into fewer warps), or a design without
  the DX epilogue on the critical path.
