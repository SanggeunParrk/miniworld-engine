# transition_fused_sm100 — round v22: profile-guided bf16 changes (contract kept)

- Date: 2026-09-26. Follows the v21 ncu profile (no saturated resource; role-pipeline latency, power-bound at real clocks).
- Critical-path check (`ab_bwd.py`, alternating): removing the DX LayerNorm-backward epilogue saves 17 µs cold / 10 µs sustained
  but only ~4 mJ — the epilogue is on the latency path. Removing one gate saves ~10 µs sustained each.

## Adopted
- `tbwd.cu` `EPIF2` (default; `-DEPI_SCALAR` restores v9): the DX epilogue's two passes in fp32x2 (x − mean times rstd as one fma,
  row sums in pair order; same rounding points for d_xn and dx). Outputs vs v9: dx rel 1.2e-5, dgamma rel 2e-7, the rest identical;
  vs the contract dx 1.6e-4, dW 4.2-4.5e-4 (unchanged class). Step 309-310 vs 317 µs sustained.
- (v21) fp32x2 gates, bit-identical.
