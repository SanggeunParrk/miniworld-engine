# transition_fused_sm100 — round v5: measurements that re-define the ceiling (no kernel speedup)

- Date: 2026-09-25
- Target kernels: `transition_fwd_sm100`, `transition_bwd_sm100`
- Baseline: v4 (`510e1531`) — forward 61.9 µs inference / backward 237.5 µs at L384. Kernels unchanged by this round (two
  experiments below were measured slower and reverted; the forward keeps them as opt-in builds).

## 1. The power cap sets the tensor ceiling on this card (`profiles/v5/clock_probe.txt`, `calib_power.json`)
The B200 here runs at a 1000 W limit. Under sustained load `nvidia-smi` reports throttle reason 0x4 (SW power cap) and:

| sustained load (L768) | SM clock | power |
|---|---:|---:|
| fused forward (this work) | ~1660-1680 MHz | ~990 W |
| fused training step (this work) | ~1860-1875 MHz | ~990 W |
| cuBLAS bf16 GEMM 8192³ | **~1100 MHz** | ~990 W |

So the tcgen05 clock peak (8188 FLOP/clk/SM × 148 × 1965 MHz = 2.38 PFLOP/s) is not attainable: dense cuBLAS measures
1.30-1.45 PFLOP/s warm (1.58 PFLOP/s was the first, cold, measurement of v1). The per-CTA exit stamps (`span_fwd.txt`) confirm it
from the kernel side: clock64 says a tile takes ~12 k cycles, globaltimer says 7.9 µs per tile — an effective ~1.55 GHz. (The v2
"tile-boundary bubble" was this clock drop, not a stall.)

**SoL definition from here on:** tensor floor at the highest dense-bf16 rate ever measured on this card, 1.58 PFLOP/s (strict: the
warm ceiling is lower); HBM floor at 7.0 TB/s. The clock-peak figure (2.38 PFLOP/s) is reported alongside.

| L384 | FLOP | floor @1.58 PF | floor @2.38 PF | v4 | SoL @1.58 | SoL @2.38 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| forward (inference) | 58.0 G | 36.7 µs | 24.3 µs | 61.9 µs | **59 %** | 39 % |
| backward (design 22·M·D·H) | 212.6 G | 134.6 µs | 89.3 µs | 237.5 µs | **57 %** | 38 % |
| training step | | 171.3 µs | 113.6 µs | 311.0 µs | **55 %** | 37 % |

SoL90 targets at L384: forward ≤ 40.8 µs, backward ≤ 149.5 µs, training step ≤ 190 µs.

## 2. MUFU and the SwiGLU element math (`sfu_bench.txt`, `ex2_test.txt`)
- MUFU: 16 op/clk/SM. The kit sigmoid (ex2 + rcp) runs at 8.0 elements/clk/SM; one FMA-pipe op at 122.
- `ex2_poly` (round-to-nearest split, degree-6 fit, integer exponent add; `src/sm100.cuh`): max relative error **7.9e-8** against
  exact exp2, below `ex2.approx.ftz`'s 1.45e-7 — not a precision reduction. The sigmoid built on it has the kit's error (3.5e-6,
  dominated by `rcp.approx`); at the bf16 `h` level 99.9998 % of values are identical.
- But `sigmoid_poly` runs at 7.2 elements/clk (issue-limited at ~18 instructions), slower than the kit's 8.0.
- In the forward, half / quarter of the exponentials on the FMA pipe measured **71.6 / 63.0 µs vs 61.8 µs** (L384 inference):
  the SwiGLU warps are latency- and issue-sensitive, not simply MUFU-bound. Kept as opt-in builds `-DSIG_POLY_HALF`,
  `-DSIG_POLY_QUARTER`; default unchanged.

## 3. Backward experiment: x in tensor memory, third `[Wa;Wb]` slot (reverted)
DH made single-buffered (released as soon as the gate has read it), x copied after each tile's last `[a|b]` from the (now single)
xn buffer into TMEM with `tcgen05.cp.128x256b` (SW128 descriptor works as the source), the freed 32 KB used as a third weight slot,
the epilogue reading x from TMEM. Correct (identical errors), but **265 µs vs 237.5**: the weight-slot wait fell, the tile-start
xn reload (654 clk) appeared, and the DX chunk stayed at ~3200 clk — now bound by the gate's instruction issue (both gate
warpgroups plus the epilogue share each SMSP: ~13 instructions per gate element).

## 4. Next ideas
1. SwiGLU in isolation (TMEM ld → math → TMEM st, no MMA) to separate its own throughput from pipeline interaction.
2. Newton-iteration reciprocal on the FMA pipe (~7 instructions, 3 iterations from a bit-trick seed) is a cheaper MUFU offload than
   `ex2_poly` (~12) — only worth it if 1. shows MUFU as the limit.
3. Under the power cap, work that is not tensor work costs clock: fewer instructions and fewer bytes moved raise the clock of the
   whole kernel.

## 5. Addendum — forward weight multicast (measured, not adopted)
The forward's 48 KB of weights per chunk, shared by the two CTAs of a 2-CTA cluster (each requests half, multicast; slots
released by both CTAs' multicast commits): correct, 63.0 / 251.4 µs vs 62.2 / 251.7 µs (L384 / L768 inference) — neutral, so
the simpler unclustered forward stays. Patch kept in `records/experiments/fwd_weight_multicast.patch`. (A no-reload ablation of the
weights gains 3 % at L384 / 8 % at L768, the ceiling for any weight-traffic change.)
