# transition_fused_sm100 — round v6: pipelined SwiGLU (forward)

- Date: 2026-09-25
- Target kernel: `transition_fwd_sm100`
- Baseline: v5 (`3de83604` / `0401300b`), forward 62.0 µs inference / 66.4 µs training-build at L384

## 1. Profile before the change
Element stages measured in isolation (`gate_bench.cu`: TMEM ld → math → TMEM st on resident data, no MMA;
`profiles/v6/gate_bench.txt`):

| stage (per 128-row chunk, 2 warpgroups) | clk | MUFU floor |
|---|---:|---:|
| backward DX gate, TMEM ld/st only | 365 | — |
| backward DX gate | 1492 | 1024 |
| backward DX gate, 16-column pipelined | 1349 | 1024 |
| forward SwiGLU | 1151 | 1024 |

The gate time is ≈ ld/st + MUFU-bound math, i.e. the two warps of an SMSP wait for their TMEM loads at the same moment and nothing
overlaps them. In the forward kernel the SwiGLU ran at ~1450 clk per chunk.

## 2. Change
Forward SwiGLU: the 64 columns of a chunk in four 16-column steps; the TMEM loads of step q + 1 are issued before step q is computed
(double buffer of 2 × 32 registers). Same arithmetic, same order. The old path stays behind `-DSWIGLU_NOPIPE`.
The same pipelining applied to the backward DX gate was neutral in the kernel (242.7 vs 240 µs — the DX chunk is bound by the
weight-slot chain, not the gate) and was reverted.

## 3. Validation
out vs contract 8.6e-5 / 8.8e-5 (L384 / L768), mismatch fraction unchanged (0.12 %), xn 1.3e-5, bit-reproducible.

## 4. Performance (CUDA-graph replay median)

| | v5 | **v6** |
|---|---:|---:|
| forward inference L384 | 62.0 µs | **57.1 µs** |
| forward training build L384 | 66.4 µs | **61.4 µs** |
| training step L384 | 312.6 µs | **304.9 µs** |
| forward training build L768 | 246.0 µs | **228.2 µs** |
| training step L768 | 1208.1 µs | **1203.2 µs** |
| forward inference L768 | 227-256 µs | 236-253 µs (noisy under the power cap) |

SoL (1.58 PF measured ceiling): forward inference 36.7 / 57.1 = **64 %** (v5 59 %).
