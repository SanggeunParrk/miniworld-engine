# transition_fused_sm100 — round v7: energy accounting under the power cap (no kernel speedup)

- Date: 2026-09-25
- Kernels unchanged from v6 (`4dec40e1`).

## 1. Energy per call (`energy.py`, NVML total-energy counter, ~3 s of back-to-back graph replays; `profiles/v7/energy.json`)

| workload | µs / call (sustained) | power | TFLOP/s | pJ / tensor FLOP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| cuBLAS 8192³ (dense GEMM, nothing else) | 843 | 981 W | **1304** | **0.75** |
| cuBLAS 147456 × 1024 × 128 (the expand shape) | 71.4 | 986 W | 542 | 1.82 |
| fused forward, inference, L384 | 63.8 | 981 W | 908 | 1.08 |
| fused training step, L384 (fwd 6·M·D·H + bwd 22·M·D·H) | 328.8 | 984 W | 823 | 1.20 |
| idle (after load) | — | 266 W | — | — |

Every workload sits at the ~985 W limit, so time per call = energy per call / ~985 W. Even a pure GEMM sustains only 1.30 PFLOP/s.
Against that sustained ceiling the forward delivers 70 % and the training step 63 %; against the 1.58 PFLOP/s best-ever
(cold) ceiling used as SoL in v5, 57 % and 52 % sustained (the 20 × 5 benchmark regime reads higher: 64 % / 56 %).

What SoL90 would require here: at the GEMM's own 0.75 pJ/FLOP, 90 % of the 1.30 PF sustained ceiling leaves ~0.08 pJ/FLOP
(~11 %) for everything that is not tensor work — the SwiGLU (two MUFU ops per hidden element), LayerNorm, the TMEM round trip of
`[a|b]` and h, smem operand traffic of 128-wide tiles, HBM I/O. The forward currently spends ~0.33 pJ/FLOP on those, the training
step ~0.45. This is the quantitative reason the power-capped card does not reach SoL90 with this op.

## 2. Experiments this round (all reverted, default unchanged)
- `rcp_nr` (bit-trick seed, three Newton steps, residual correction): max relative error 6.0e-8, below `rcp.approx`'s 9.7e-8
  (`nr_test.txt`). In isolation the kit sigmoid with half its reciprocals on the FMA pipe runs at 9.4 elements/clk vs 8.0
  (`sfu_bench.txt`). In the forward kernel: 60.8 µs vs 57.2 — slower, like `ex2_poly` in v5. Under the cap, extra instructions cost
  more than the MUFU time they free.
- `__nanosleep` back-off in the polling weight producers (forward and backward): 57.5 / 246.8 µs vs 57.2 / 240 — neutral/worse.

## 3. Where further gains could still come from (ranked by expected energy saved)
1. 2-CTA (cta_group::2) MMA in the forward: the expand's `[Wa;Wb]` and the squeeze's Ws split cleanly by N across the pair, halving
   per-SM B-operand smem reads and L2 weight traffic and halving MMA instructions — the operand-traffic advantage cuBLAS's 256-wide
   tiles have over our 128-wide ones.
2. Backward: the design's recompute (22 vs 16 M·D·H) is 27 % of its tensor energy; it is part of the H100 algorithm this port keeps.
