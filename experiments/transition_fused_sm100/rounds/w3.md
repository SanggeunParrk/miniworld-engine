# transition_fused_sm100 — widths round w3: the wide backward's gate, and the no-recompute path (measured; nothing adopted)

- Date: 2026-09-29. The user allowed dropping the fp32 recompute of a / b in the backward. B200 GPU 7, L384 unless noted, µs.

## 1. What bounds tgate_w (ablations, `-DABL_STORE`, `-DABL_EPI`, `-DSTORE_SMALL`)

| D | full | no gate math | no stores | neither | stores into an L2-resident 1024-row target |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 256 | 317 | 358 | 216 | 205 (1.13 PF/s) | 284-289 |
| 384 | 736 | 819 | 440 | 459 | — |
| 512 | 1253 | 1259 | 884 | 730 (1.27 PF/s) | 1008-1038 |

The stores (h, dA, dB: 48 KB per 64-unit item) cost 30-40 %; the gate math nothing. About half of the store cost remains when the target
is L2-resident: the stores share the SM's TMA traffic budget with the loads (with no stores the loads run at ~52 B/clk per SM), the
rest is the HBM write path. Not the layout: [chunk][M][64] outputs (8 KB contiguous boxes) change it by 4 %.

Tried, all slower or neutral (outputs bit-identical throughout):

| variant | D256 | D384 | D512 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| tgate_w (4-stage ring, one staging buffer) | 315-318 | 683-705 | 1231-1250 |
| gate math before the staging wait | 353-360 | 727-738 | 1225-1233 |
| double staging, 2-stage ring | 568-571 | 1042-1049 | 1681 |
| `tgate2_w`: two chunks per A load (dh N 128, a|b N 256, one 384-column accumulator, 2-stage ring) | 506 | 890 | 1356 |

tgate2_w halves the A re-streaming but loses the accumulator double buffer (the next pair's MMAs wait for the epilogue's two passes)
and the ring depth (shared memory: 2 x 56 KB + 96 KB staging).

## 2. No recompute: the forward saves a, b (bf16) — `bench_wstep.py`

Forward `tswiglu_w -DSAVE_AB` writes h, a, b; backward dh = bf16(dy Ws) (cuBLAS) -> `tgate_elem.cu` (elementwise h, dA, dB from dh, a, b)
-> the rest as w2.

Accuracy vs the fp32 module (1920 rows to L768): out 2.2e-3, dx 3.3e-3, dW 3.9e-3, dgamma / dbeta 3.4-4.4e-3 -- below PyTorch's own bf16
module (3.3e-3 / 4.1e-3 / 5.3e-3 / 5.0-5.8e-3); bit-reproducible.

| step | recompute (w2) | no recompute | torch.compile |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| D384 L384 | ~2150 | 2113 | 2279 |
| D384 L768 | ~8640 | 8530 | 9425 |
| D512 L384 | ~3496 | 3403 | 3508 |
| D512 L768 | ~14035 | 13293 | 14538 |

D512 L384 parts: forward 1039 (856 without the a / b saves), dh 220, elementwise gate 625 (5.8 TB/s: at the HBM limit), dWs 240,
dWab 627, d_xn 502, LN backward 186. The HBM traffic of a / b (written in the forward, read in the backward) and of dh eats the saved
tensor work: 1-5 % faster for +2 M H bf16 of activation memory per layer (1.2 GB at D512 L384). Not adopted as the default; the
recompute path keeps the H100 contract and no extra memory.

## 3. Standing for D >= 256

At these widths the op is tensor work plus the HBM traffic of its [M][4D] intermediates, at the card's power cap; the backward is within
~10 % of torch.compile either way. What would move it structurally is keeping h / dA / dB on chip (the dW products accumulated inside the
gate kernel), which the TMEM budget does not allow above D = 256 (dWab alone is D columns per CTA).
