# transition_fused_sm100 — widths round w5: the D >= 384 step is at this algorithm's energy floor (measured; nothing adopted)

- Date: 2026-09-29. User: dig further. B200 GPU 7, L384, µs. A/B within one process, 4 alternating rounds (run-to-run noise across
  processes is ~+-10 %).

## 1. xn resident in the SwiGLU forward (`src/tswiglu2_w.cu`)

Hypothesis: tswiglu_w re-loads the xn tile for every 128-unit item, so its loads ask ~64 B/clk of the SM's ~52 B/clk TMA intake, and the
h / a / b stores compete for the rest. Variant: the 128-row xn tile resident (loaded once per tile, all H / 128 items reuse it), only the
weights through the ring; staging per warpgroup (D384, 2-stage ring) or one shared buffer (D512, 3-stage ring). Outputs bit-identical.

| | tswiglu_w (-DSAVE_AB) | tswiglu2_w |
|---|---:|---:|
| D384 | 400-419 (median 411) | 570-588 (583) |
| D512 | 629-709 (697) | 745-763 (755) |

Slower: the intake hypothesis was wrong (and the per-tile xn reload is a bubble).

## 2. Energy accounting of the D512 L384 training step (the card runs at its 1000 W cap: time ~= energy / 985 W)

- Tensor work: forward 6 M D H + backward 12 M D H (dh 2, dWs 2, [dWa; dWb] 4, d_xn 4) = 2.78 TFLOP; at the cuBLAS-level ~0.75 pJ/FLOP
  (v7 / v12 measurements, operand movement included) ~2.1 J.
- HBM traffic: forward ~3.0 GB (x, xn, h / a / b written, h and x read, out), backward ~6.5 GB (a / b read, dA / dB written and read
  twice, h / dy / xn read, d_xn, dx) = ~9.5 GB at ~95 pJ/B average (read 115, write 72) ~0.9 J.
- Sum ~3.0 J -> ~3.0 ms at 985 W; measured 3.06-3.08 ms.

So the step is at the energy floor of this algorithm on this card; overlap changes (staging depth, ring depth, residency) cannot move it,
which is what every w3-w5 overlap experiment showed. Only removing bytes or FLOPs can:

| removal | saving | why it is out of reach at D >= 384 |
|---|---|---|
| read [dA|dB] once (dWab and d_xn fused) | -1.2 GB read, ~-140 µs | a CTA would hold the d_xn accumulator (D columns) and a dWab chunk (D columns): > 512 TMEM columns |
| never write h (squeeze fused into the SwiGLU) | -0.6 GB write + read, ~-110 µs | the output accumulator is D columns, plus [a|b]: 512 is full at D = 512; possible at D = 384 (~2-3 %) |
| no a / b (recompute) | 2.4 GB of traffic ~230 mJ | replaced by 4 M D H of MMA ~300 mJ: a wash (w3 §2) |

## 3. Standing

D384 / D512 training steps: 1.04-1.09x torch.compile (module bench), 1.2x torch.compile (same script); forward 1.4-1.6x. Further gains at
these widths need a different algorithm (lower-precision intermediates, which the user keeps out of the default) rather than scheduling.
