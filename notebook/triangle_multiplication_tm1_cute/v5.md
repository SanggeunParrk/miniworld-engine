## 2. What fixed the v3/v4 launch deadlock (UMMA never retired)
v3/v4 established (by bisection) that the tcgen05 UMMA (`cute.gemm`) never retired despite
operand/acc/tmem construction matching quack, memcheck+synccheck clean, mbarrier semantics
verified. The fix came from porting the canonical minimal example
`examples/python/CuTeDSL/cute/blackwell/kernel/dense_gemm/dense_gemm.py` (fetched from the
CUTLASS repo; not committed). The specific structural elements a bare hand-rolled kernel was
missing, all required for the UMMA to retire:
1. **AB operands driven through `PipelineTmaUmma` handle API** (`.create(...).make_participants()`
   -> `ab_producer.acquire_and_advance()` gives the TMA-load barrier; the TMA copy uses
   `tma_bar_ptr=handle.barrier`; the MMA warp calls `ab_consumer.wait_and_advance()` BEFORE
   `cute.gemm` and `handle.release()` after). A bare mbarrier + `fence_view_async_shared` was
   insufficient — the UMMA must consume AB through the TMA-UMMA pipeline handshake.
2. **`pipeline_init_arrive(cluster_shape_mn=..., is_relaxed=True)` + `pipeline_init_wait(...)`**
   bracketing the smem/partition setup, with `defer_sync=True` on both pipelines.
3. **`tmem.relinquish_alloc_permit()` AFTER the MMA** (not right after allocate), all threads.
4. **`pipeline.sync(barrier_id=1)` before `tmem.free`**; tmem retrieve barrier is NamedBarrier id 0.
5. accumulator via `PipelineUmmaAsync` state API: `producer_commit(state)` after the MMA (no
   `producer_acquire` needed for a fresh single-stage buffer), `consumer_wait(state)` by all
   threads before the epilogue.
`cute.gemm` itself is issued at WARP level (not `elect_one`; it elects internally) with
`tiled_mma.set(tcgen05.Field.ACCUMULATE, k!=0)`, and the acc fragment is
`make_fragment_C(partition_shape_C(mma_tiler[:2]))` (no appended stage).

## 3. Kernel structure (single CTA, single warpgroup = 128 threads)
- Warp 0: TMA load (producer) + MMA (consumer of AB, producer of acc).
- All 128 threads: TMEM alloc/retrieve, acc consumer_wait, epilogue (TMEM->reg->smem->TMA store per epi-subtile).
- TMA atoms: `cluster_shape_to_tma_atom_A/B` + `make_tiled_tma_atom_A/B`; 4-mode staged smem
  layouts; epilogue per-epi-subtile TMA store.
