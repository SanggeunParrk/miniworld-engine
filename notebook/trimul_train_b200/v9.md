# trimul_train_b200 v9 — route LN dw/db reductions through arch-agnostic optimized LN bwd -> BEATS cuequiv at ALL L

## Goal
Kill the L=384 training loss: v8 nsys showed the LN weight/bias-grad reductions done as torch
`.sum(0)` (`reduce_kernel`, ~490us = 37.5% of the L=384 graph step) were the last big cost.
Route them through the repo's existing arch-agnostic optimized LayerNorm backward
(`kernels/layernorm`, compile_native atomic/partial/persistent dispatch) instead of torch.

## Change (training_b200.py)
- **LN_in bwd WHOLESALE**: replaced the split (triton `_ln_bwd_dx` for dx + torch `.sum(0)` for
  dw/db) with `compile_native._dispatch_bwd(dy, x, weight, mean, rstd)` -> (dx, dw, db) in one
  fused, dispatch-cached triton call. The fused LN fwd (`_ln_fwd_kernel`) now also emits `mean`
  (fp32); forward saves (pair, mean_in, rstd_in) instead of xhat_in for LN_in. Arch-agnostic,
  self-tunes per-GPU (atomic/partial/persistent) via the dispatch cache on B200.
- **LN_out dw/db**: dx stays fused in `dgrad_lnbwd_sm100` (v4), so d_out_n is never materialized.
  The affine grads keep the T-trick (T=d_proj^T@xhat_out), but the slow `db_proj = d_proj.sum(0)`
  (M,D)->(D,) torch reduce is replaced by `_colsum` = a coalesced cuBLAS GEMV (ones @ d_proj),
  fp32 acc. (dWln_out=(Wpt*T).sum(0) is (D,D)->(D,), negligible, left as torch.)
- LN stats fp32 throughout (constraint). dispatch cache lets B200 pick the reduction path.
